# claude-projects

| Project | Description |
|---|---|
| [fl26-broadcast-director](fl26-broadcast-director/) | Football Life 26 sider mod: a self-directing Fan View camera with presets per stadium and competition, live direction (closing minutes, extra time, penalty shoot-outs), smooth transitions and subtle hand-held movement |
| [fl26-tournament-engine](fl26-tournament-engine/) | Football Life 26 sider mod that runs formats the PES 2021 engine can't: the 36-team Champions League league phase and the 48-team World Cup 2026, plus custom groups and leagues. It makes the draws, simulates other matches, records yours from the scoreboard, and writes tables, brackets and an HTML report |
| [fl26-formbook](fl26-formbook/) | Football Life 26 sider mod: records every result automatically, shows head-to-head and form before each match, and can adjust difficulty based on your results |
| [fl26-matchday-conditions](fl26-matchday-conditions/) | Football Life 26 sider mod that picks weather, day or night kick-offs and pitch season to suit the month and climate |
